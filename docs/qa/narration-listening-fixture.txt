Chapter XII

Dr. Rao waited beside the window. At 3.14 p.m., the bell rang.

“Don’t wake him,” Maya whispered softly. Arjun nodded.
“Hello!” she shouted across the field, smiling at her brother.
“Get out!” he shouted angrily, pointing at the door.

Maya read the report. “It says he shouted angrily yesterday,” she said calmly.
“Fine,” Arjun said, not angrily. “We can wait.”

‘My parents’ house,’ Maya said. ‘That’s where we should go.’

“Yes.”
“No.”
“Maybe.”

* * *

He ran, rushed, and slammed the gate behind him. “Stop—”
The words caught in his throat. “Please… let me explain.”

“What a wonderful surprise,” Maya said, staring at the broken window.
Arjun lowered his eyes. “I’ll make it right,” he said gently.
She did not answer. The room was quiet.

He unfolded a letter containing the words “I am not afraid.” He read it twice.
The next morning, they walked to the house together.
